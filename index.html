Why Transcript Lake

Your team already solved it.
Now the answer is findable.

01

Capture continuously

A supervised, event-driven stream follows complete local transcript appends. Durable byte cursors resume without rescanning settled history.

02

Mask before storage

Credential-shaped and high-entropy text is replaced before an event reaches the canonical partition or any downstream projection.

03

Query one contract

Provider-neutral events make sessions, tools, tokens, hook decisions, labels, and cross-runtime signals available through DuckDB.

04

Keep authority local

Vendor stores remain read-only. NDJSON stays authoritative. Parquet and Oko projections are explicit, rebuildable derived state.

A narrow trust boundary

Raw on one side.
Masked on the other.

Transcript Lake gives every downstream consumer the same normalized evidence without making each one handle raw vendor files, secrets, or incompatible schemas.
